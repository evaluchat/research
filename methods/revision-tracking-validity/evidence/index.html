Method — Revision-tracking validity (evidence collection)

One evidence collection per method. No evidence contributions yet: the method is implementation-blocked pending the general Canvas condition-allocation capability. A public synthetic acceptance demonstration may be the first contribution only after the capability exists; it is not student evidence.

When evidence arrives, each contribution is one completed Markdown packet under methods/revision-tracking-validity/evidence/<slug>.md, rendered from this version's evidence template. The packet preserves the shared roles in shared evidence roles, links the pre-existing revision-tracking validity research question, and records method: {id, version, levers, canvas} in its system-authored provenance.

Every contribution must preserve the method boundary: no raw student text, AI transcript, identifiable revision trace, identity-to-condition mapping, integrity verdict, or grade-changing use. The packet's observations and results sections record measurements; the reflection section contains interpretation; the limitations section records uncertainty, scope, and confounders.
