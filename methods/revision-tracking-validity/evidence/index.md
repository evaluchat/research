# Method — Revision-tracking validity (evidence collection)

One evidence collection per method. No evidence contributions yet: the method is
[implementation-blocked](https://github.com/openrigor/research/issues/1) pending the general
Canvas condition-allocation capability. A public synthetic acceptance demonstration may be the
first contribution only after the capability exists; it is not student evidence.

When evidence arrives, each contribution is one completed Markdown packet under
`methods/revision-tracking-validity/evidence/<slug>.md`, rendered from this version's
[evidence template](../evidence-template.en.md). The packet preserves the shared roles in
[shared evidence roles](../../../governance/evidence-roles.en.md), links the pre-existing
[revision-tracking validity research question](../../../theory/revision-tracking-validity-question.en.md),
and records `method: {id, version, levers, canvas}` in its system-authored provenance.

Every contribution must preserve the method boundary: no raw student text, AI transcript,
identifiable revision trace, identity-to-condition mapping, integrity verdict, or grade-changing
use. The packet's observations and results sections record measurements; the reflection
section contains interpretation; the limitations section records uncertainty, scope, and
confounders.
