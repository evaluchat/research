Method — AI Assignment Stress Test (evidence collection)

One evidence collection per method. No evidence contributions yet.

When evidence arrives, each contribution is one completed Markdown packet under methods/ai-assignment-stress-test/evidence/<slug>.md, rendered from this version's evidence template. The packet preserves the shared roles in shared evidence roles, links the pre-existing research question, and records method: {id, version, levers, canvas} in its system-authored provenance, including the pinned model set.

The collection accepts assignment-level, teacher-approved reports only. It contains no raw learner material, student process telemetry, authorship verdicts, or AI-use detections. Any published assignment artefact must be appropriately anonymised and its consent and retention record included in provenance.
