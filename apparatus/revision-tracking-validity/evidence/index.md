# Apparatus — Revision-tracking validity (evidence collection)

One evidence collection per apparatus. No evidence contributions yet: the apparatus is
[implementation-blocked](https://github.com/evaluchat/research/issues/1) pending the general
Canvas condition-allocation capability. A public synthetic acceptance demonstration may be the
first contribution only after the capability exists; it is not student evidence.

When evidence arrives, each contribution is a mini-bundle under
`apparatus/revision-tracking-validity/evidence/<slug>/` (see the
[evidence-bundle template](../../../templates/evidence-bundle/index.md)). It must link the
pre-existing [revision-tracking validity research question](../../../theory/revision-tracking-validity-question.en.md)
and record `apparatus: {id, version, configuration}` and `canvas: {version}` in provenance.

Every contribution must preserve the apparatus boundary: no raw student text, AI transcript,
identifiable revision trace, identity-to-condition mapping, integrity verdict, or grade-changing
use. `observations.md` and `results.md` record measurements; `reflection.md` contains
interpretation; `limitations.md` records uncertainty, scope, and confounders.
